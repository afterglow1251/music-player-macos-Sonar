Inside Sonar

Three things it does better than the rest

A player you look at as much as you listen to. Pick one and have a play: everything below is live.

See the song, not just hear it

The classic stacked tiles, drawn live from the music you're playing, with peaks that fall under gravity. Winamp spirit, built for today's Mac.

  • Three modes: spectrum, oscilloscope and Bloom, a MilkDrop-style kaleidoscope. Double-click to switch.
  • Ten themes, or one that takes its colours from the album art.
  • Artwork that breathes with the bass.

Every letter, right on the beat

Synced lyrics show up on their own. One click and ElevenLabs times them word by word, down to the letter a singer holds, so the fill follows the voice.

  • Found automatically and kept next to the song, so they work offline.
  • Words or letters: switch how the fill moves, live.
  • Wrong song? Paste a link or drop in a lyrics file.
  • DJ mixes too: every track in a set gets its own lyrics.

Paste a link, it's yours

Drop a YouTube link into Sonar and it lands in your library as a proper track, with cover art and tags. Queue a few more while it works.

  • Queue as many as you like: paste, press space, paste the next.
  • Long mixes keep their chapters, so you can jump between tracks.
  • Drag and drop files or links onto the window.

Get Sonar

Free, and yours in a minute

No account, no subscription. Download it, drop it into Applications, and press play.

1

Download

Get the latest version of Sonar and unzip it.

2

Move to Applications

Drag Sonar.app into your Applications folder.

3

Let it open

macOS blocks apps from outside the App Store the first time. Paste this into Terminal once, then open Sonar as usual:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Sonar.app

Optional

Turn on YouTube downloads

Playing your own music works right away. To download from YouTube, Sonar needs two free helper tools. It takes a couple of minutes, once.

  1. Install Homebrew, the Mac's installer for tools like these. Open Terminal (⌘ Space, type "Terminal"), paste this and press Return:
    /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"
    Already have it? Skip ahead. Details at brew.sh.
  2. Install the download tools with one more command:
    brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg
  3. Restart Sonar, paste a YouTube link, and it lands in your library.
Optional

Turn on letter-perfect karaoke

Line-synced lyrics work without any setup. To time them to the letter, or to get lyrics for songs that have none, connect your ElevenLabs key.

  1. Create an ElevenLabs account at elevenlabs.io.
  2. Make an API key in Settings → API Keys. Give it access to Forced Alignment and Speech to Text. Nothing else is needed.
  3. Paste it into Sonar: Settings → ElevenLabs. It's kept in your Keychain.
  4. Open the lyrics of any song and press Sync with ElevenLabs.
