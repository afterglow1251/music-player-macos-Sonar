Visualizer

See the song, not just hear it.

A real FFT on the audio you're playing, drawn as the classic stacked tiles with peaks that fall under gravity. Winamp spirit, rebuilt natively.

  • Three modes. Spectrum, oscilloscope and Bloom, a MilkDrop-style kaleidoscope. Double-click to flip.
  • Ten themes, or one that takes its colours from the album art.
  • Artwork that breathes with the bass, over a backdrop tinted by the cover.
Karaoke

Every letter, right on the beat.

Synced lyrics show up on their own. One click times them word by word, down to the letter a singer holds, so the fill follows the voice, not a guess.

  • Lyrics found automatically from LRCLIB, and cached next to the song so they work offline.
  • Timed to the letter with ElevenLabs: about a cent per song, with your own key.
  • Wrong song? Paste a link or drop in an .lrc file instead.
  • DJ mixes too: each chapter of a mix gets its own lyrics.

YouTube → Library

Paste a link. It's yours.

Drop a YouTube URL into Sonar and it lands in your library as a proper track, with cover art and tags. Queue a few more while it works.

  • Queue as many as you like: paste, press space, paste the next.
  • Long mixes keep their chapters, so the seek bar shows every track in the set.
  • Drag and drop audio files or links onto the window.

And the rest

Built like a Mac app should be.

Small, fast and quiet on your battery, with the little details a Mac app should have.

Waveform seek bar

The song's real shape. Scrub with a drag or a swipe, and hover to see the time.

Menu bar mini player

Play, skip and seek from the menu bar, even over full-screen apps.

10-band equalizer

Presets or your own curve, previewed live on the visualizer.

Two columns when it fits

Full screen or a big window spreads out into now playing plus library.

Playlists and favorites

Hand-made playlists, a favorites filter and multi-select for bulk edits.

Sleep timer

Stop after 15 to 60 minutes, a custom time, or at the end of the track.

Keyboard-first

Media keys, Now Playing in Control Center, and shortcuts for everything.

Picks up where you left off

Same track, same second, every time you open it.

Plays what you have

MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF and FLAC, straight from a folder you choose.

Get Sonar

Free. Open source. Yours.

01

Download

Grab Sonar.zip and unzip it. Works on any Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later.

02

Move to Applications

Drag Sonar.app into your Applications folder.

03

First launch

It's a beta that isn't notarized yet, so right-click → Open the first time. On macOS 15+, use Open Anyway in Privacy & Security.

Download for macOS

Turn on YouTube downloads

Playing your own music works right away. To download from YouTube, Sonar needs two free helper tools. It takes a couple of minutes, once.

  1. Install Homebrew, the Mac's app installer for tools like these. Open Terminal (⌘ Space, type "Terminal"), paste this and press Return:
    /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"
    Already have it? Skip to the next step. Details at brew.sh.
  2. Install the download tools with one more command:
    brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg
  3. Restart Sonar, paste a YouTube link, and it lands in your library.
